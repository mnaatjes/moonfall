# Reference: Historic Lunar Missions

This reference document compiles details on major historic lunar missions, including both crewed and robotic endeavors. It outlines their landing or orbit locations, scientific payloads, and mission durations, which can serve as design blueprints for historical scenario maps and campaign objectives.

---

## Historic Lunar Missions Database

| Mission Name | Country | Type | Location (Target) | Duration | Key Science Payloads |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Luna 9** (1966) | USSR | Robotic Land | Oceanus Procellarum | 3 days | TV camera, radiation detector. |
| **Surveyor 1** (1966) | USA | Robotic Land | Oceanus Procellarum | 6 weeks | TV camera, temperature/strain sensors. |
| **Apollo 11** (1969) | USA | Crewed Land | Mare Tranquillitatis | 8.1 days | EASEP (passive seismometer, laser ranging retroreflector, solar wind collector). |
| **Apollo 15** (1971) | USA | Crewed Land | Hadley–Apennine | 12.3 days | ALSEP, Lunar Roving Vehicle (LRV), sub-satellite magnetometers, mapping cameras. |
| **Apollo 17** (1972) | USA | Crewed Land | Taurus-Littrow valley | 12.6 days | ALSEP (gravimeter, mass spectrometer, seismic profiling), LRV, deep-core drills. |
| **Luna 24** (1976) | USSR | Robotic Sample | Mare Crisium | 12 days | Rotary drill rig, soil sample return capsule. |
| **Lunar Prospector** (1998) | USA | Robotic Orbit | Polar Orbit (100km alt) | 1.5 years | Gamma-Ray Spectrometer (GRS), Neutron Spectrometer (NS), Magnetometer (MAG). |
| **LRO** (2009–Present) | USA | Robotic Orbit | Polar Orbit (50km alt) | 17+ years | LOLA (Topography), LROC (Cameras), CRaTER (Radiation), LEND (Neutron detector). |
| **Chang'e 3** (2013) | China | Robotic Land | Mare Imbrium | 11+ years | Yutu Rover (Ground Penetrating Radar), UV telescope, soil probe. |
| **Chang'e 4** (2019) | China | Robotic Land | Von Kármán crater (Far Side) | 7+ years | Yutu-2 Rover, Lunar Lander Neutron & Dosimetry (LND), Low-frequency Radio Spectrometer. |
| **Chandrayaan-3** (2023) | India | Robotic Land | Polar region (69.37°S) | 14 days | Pragyan Rover, ChaSTE (thermal probe), ILSA (seismometer), APXS (X-ray spectrometer). |
| **SLIM** (2024) | Japan | Robotic Land | Shioli crater | 8 months | Lunar Excursion Vehicles (LEV-1 & LEV-2), Multi-band Camera (MBC) for mineral composition. |
| **Chang'e 6** (2024) | China | Robotic Sample | South Pole-Aitken basin | 53 days | Soil drill, core sample collector, landing camera. |

## Application for Scenario Mapping
These historic parameters provide ready-made metrics to define map properties in game design:
*   **Luna & Surveyor Sites:** Excellent for early-game campaign settings where the player must salvage historic probes or set up bases in flat, stable basalt plains (*maria*).
*   **Apollo Sites:** High-science zones that can yield permanent boosts to tech progression or serve as heritage protection zones.
*   **South Pole Sites (Chandrayaan/SLIM/Chang'e):** Late-game, high-difficulty maps featuring steep crater walls, low-angle shadows, and high concentrations of water ice resources.
