# Profiles: Lunar Resources

This document compiles scientific details, chemical compositions, and historical context for resources available for extraction on the Moon.

---

## Water Ice

*   **Chemical Formula / Composition:** $H_2O$ (comprising hydrogen and oxygen)
*   **Narrative Profile:** 
    Concentrated inside polar Permanently Shadowed Regions (PSRs) where temperatures stay below -200°C (-328°F). Water ice is the most critical resource for long-term survival, as it can be melted and split into oxygen for breathing and liquid hydrogen/oxygen rocket propellant.
*   **In-Game Concept:** High-value logistics resource. Essential for refueling launchers and maintaining life support parameters.

---

## Titanium (Ilmenite)

*   **Chemical Formula / Composition:** $FeTiO_3$ (Iron-Titanium Oxide)
*   **Narrative Profile:** 
    Abundant in the dark volcanic plains (maria) of the Moon. Smelting ilmenite yields high-strength titanium alloys for construction, iron, and oxygen as a byproduct. High concentrations are found in regions like Mare Tranquillitatis.
*   **In-Game Concept:** Basic base construction material. Used to fabricate habitat hulls and structural beams.

---

## Helium-3

*   **Chemical Formula / Composition:** $^3He$ (Helium isotope)
*   **Narrative Profile:** 
    Deposited into the lunar soil over billions of years by the solar wind (Earth's magnetic field blocks it from reaching our planet). Helium-3 is a fuel source for clean, non-radioactive nuclear fusion reactors.
*   **In-Game Concept:** High-value export material. Exporting Helium-3 back to Earth generates massive financial funding.

---

## Silicon

*   **Chemical Formula / Composition:** $SiO_2$ (Silicates / Silicon Dioxide)
*   **Narrative Profile:** 
    Highly abundant in lunar regolith (the fine rock dust covering the surface). Silicon is readily extracted using solar furnaces and is the raw feedstock for manufacturing solar panels and basic computer semiconductors.
*   **In-Game Concept:** Energy generation feed. Used to expand localized base power systems.
