# Profiles: Spacecraft

This document provides historical backgrounds, functional descriptions, and in-game profiles for the spacecraft hulls available to the player.

---

## Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO)

*   **Operator:** NASA (United States)
*   **Operational Era:** 2009–Present
*   **Narrative Profile:** 
    Launched to map the lunar surface in unprecedented resolution, the LRO has compiled a giant repository of high-resolution visual imagery, elevation maps, and mineral data. Its LOLA altimeter and LROC cameras mapped the landing spots for Apollo missions and identified resource-rich regions near the south pole.
*   **In-Game Concept:** The primary high-altitude surveyor. Equipped with high-power slots for long-range mapping instruments.

---

## Luna Robotic Land Probe

*   **Operator:** USSR / Russia
*   **Operational Era:** 1966–1976
*   **Narrative Profile:** 
    Representing early Soviet robotic missions like Luna 9 (the first spacecraft to achieve a survivable landing on another celestial body) and Luna 24 (which successfully drilled and returned soil samples). These probes established that the lunar soil could support landers without sinking.
*   **In-Game Concept:** A cheap, stationary lander designed for localized surface surveys and soil core drills.

---

## Pragyan Micro-Rover

*   **Operator:** ISRO (India)
*   **Operational Era:** 2023 (Chandrayaan-3)
*   **Narrative Profile:** 
    A compact, 26 kg solar-powered micro-rover that successfully explored the high-latitude southern region of the Moon. Pragyan confirmed the presence of sulfur, aluminum, calcium, iron, and silicon in the regolith using its spectroscopic payloads.
*   **In-Game Concept:** Extremely low cost and low mass. Best suited for short-range exploration of newly discovered landing spots.

---

## Yutu Heavy Rover

*   **Operator:** CNSA (China)
*   **Operational Era:** 2013–Present (Chang'e 3 and Chang'e 4)
*   **Narrative Profile:** 
    The Yutu (Jade Rabbit) rovers explored Mare Imbrium (Yutu) and Von Kármán crater on the Lunar far side (Yutu-2). Equipped with ground-penetrating radar, Yutu mapped the subsurface layers of the Moon down to depths of several hundred meters.
*   **In-Game Concept:** High-end explorer. Features massive instrument capabilities and subsurface radar functions.

---

## Apollo Lunar Module (LM)

*   **Operator:** NASA (United States)
*   **Operational Era:** 1969–1972
*   **Narrative Profile:** 
    The first crewed spacecraft designed to operate solely in the vacuum of space. The Lunar Module consisted of a descent stage (which acted as the landing platform) and an ascent stage (which housed the crew and returned them to lunar orbit). 
*   **In-Game Concept:** Late-game mobile habitat/landing vehicle. Essential for establishing early crewed outposts.
