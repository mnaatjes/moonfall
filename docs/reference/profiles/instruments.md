# Profiles: Scientific Instruments

This document contains descriptions, historical operational contexts, and gameplay functions for the scientific instruments equipped on spacecraft.

---

## LOLA Altimeter

*   **Host Mission:** Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO)
*   **Narrative Profile:** 
    The Lunar Orbiter Laser Altimeter (LOLA) determines the shape of the Moon by sending laser pulses down to the surface and measuring the return time. This data has built the most precise topographic models of the Moon, allowing scientists to identify safe landing sites and map deep, permanently shadowed polar craters.
*   **In-Game Concept:** Reveals high-resolution elevation and slope maps. Essential for finding flat spots to build bases.

---

## LROC Wide Angle Camera (WAC)

*   **Host Mission:** Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO)
*   **Narrative Profile:** 
    The Wide Angle Camera captures images in seven different color bands, covering the visible and ultraviolet spectrum. By measuring how different regions reflect light, scientists use LROC data to calculate titanium and iron abundances across basaltic plains.
*   **In-Game Concept:** Visual map rendering. Unlocks titanium ore overlays on the scanned grid.

---

## LEND Neutron Detector

*   **Host Mission:** Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO)
*   **Narrative Profile:** 
    The Lunar Exploration Neutron Detector (LEND) measures neutron radiation escaping from the lunar soil. Neutrons are slowed down by hydrogen atoms; by tracking regions with low neutron emission (neutron suppression), LEND maps water ice reserves inside cold polar traps.
*   **In-Game Concept:** Low-fidelity, wide-radius resource locator. Reveals water ice locations, but with high positional uncertainty.

---

## CRaTER Sensor

*   **Host Mission:** Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter (LRO)
*   **Narrative Profile:** 
    The Cosmic Ray Telescope for the Effects of Radiation (CRaTER) measures galactic cosmic rays and solar particle events. It helps characterize the radiation environment that astronauts will face, guiding the design of shielding systems.
*   **In-Game Concept:** Hazard sensor. Maps solar and cosmic radiation danger zones.

---

## ChaSTE Probe

*   **Host Mission:** Chandrayaan-3 (Pragyan Rover/Vikram Lander)
*   **Narrative Profile:** 
    The Chandra's Surface Thermophysical Experiment (ChaSTE) measures the thermal conductivity and temperature of the top 10 cm of lunar soil. During its deployment, it recorded the first vertical thermal gradient profile at the lunar south pole.
*   **In-Game Concept:** Direct surface probe. Reveals 100% accurate resource composition within a small localized landing area.
