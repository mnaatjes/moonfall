# Reference: Lunar Launch Economy (Cost/KG to TLI)

This reference document compiles historical and present-day economic data for payload delivery to Trans-Lunar Injection (TLI). It details launch costs, capacities, and unit pricing per kilogram (both unadjusted and adjusted for inflation to 2026 USD) across different nations and operational models (government vs. commercial).

## Economic Rationale for Colonization Scenarios
When designing the resource economy for a lunar colonization game:
*   **Government Era (Saturn V / N1):** Extremely high cost barrier. Early missions are entirely state-funded prestige operations with tiny scientific payload capacities.
*   **Transition Era (Proton / Long March 5):** Moderately high cost, allowing for orbital probes and basic robotic sample collection, but still prohibitive for crewed base construction.
*   **Commercial/Modern Era (Falcon Heavy / Starship):** Significant reduction in cost per kilogram to TLI. This economic shift is what makes commercial cargo delivery, ISRU (in-situ resource utilization), and permanent habitat construction mathematically viable.
